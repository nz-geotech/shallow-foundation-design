4. Calculation Basis

4.1 Jibson (2007) method

log₁₀(DN) = −2.710 + log₁₀[(1 − ac/amax)2.335 × (ac/amax)−1.478] + 0.424 Mw + 0.454 Zn

DN is calculated in centimetres and converted to millimetres (×10). Zn = Φ⁻¹(1 − p/100). The supplied ±0.454 term is implemented as +0.454 Zn, so exceedance probability controls the prediction.

4.2 Ambraseys & Menu (1988) method

log₁₀(u₁) = 0.90 + 2.53 log₁₀(1 − kc/km) − 1.09 log₁₀(kc/km) + 0.30 Zn

Using kc/km = ac/amax; u₁ in centimetres, converted to mm (×10). Valid for 0 < kc/km < 1.

4.3 Ambraseys & Srbulov (1995) method

log₁₀(u₁) = −2.41 + 0.47 Ms − 0.010 r + 2.64 log₁₀(1 − q) − 1.02 log₁₀(q) + 0.58 Zn

q = ac/amax; r = De (km). u₁ is assumed in cm and converted to mm (×10).

4.4 Anderson et al. (2008) — NCHRP Report 611

D (mm) = 25.4 × 10−1.51 − 0.74 log₁₀(r) + 3.27 log₁₀(1 − r) − 0.8 log₁₀(amax) + 1.59 log₁₀(25.4 Vo) + 0.23 Zn, where r = ac/amax and Vo is the independently entered peak ground velocity (m/sec).

4.5 Bray & Travasarou (2007) method

ln(D) = −1.10 − 2.83 ln(ky) − 0.333[ln(ky)]² + 0.566 ln(ky) ln[Sa(1.5Ts)] + 3.04 ln[Sa(1.5Ts)] − 0.244{ln[Sa(1.5Ts)]}² + 1.5Ts + 0.278(M − 7) + 0.66Zn.

ky = ac (g); Ts = 4H/Vs. For Ts < 0.05 s the spreadsheet intercept −0.22 is used. When rigid block is checked, Ts, 1.5Ts and 1.3Ts are set to zero and Sa(1.5Ts) is replaced by amax. Bray and Travasarou (2007) method ignores any insignificant displacement <10 mm.

4.6 Wood & Elms — RRU Bulletin 84

D (mm) = [3 × Vo² / (amax × 9.81)] × [1 / (ac/amax) + (ac/amax) − 2] × 1000

4.7 Bray & Macedo (2023) — scalar hazard

ln(D/cm) = a₀ + a₁ ln(ky) + a₂[ln(ky)]² + a₃ ln(ky) ln[Sa(1.3Ts)] + a₄ ln[Sa(1.3Ts)] + a₅[ln(Sa(1.3Ts))]² + a₆Ts + a₇Ts² + a₈M.

The final probability-adjusted displacement is multiplied by 10 to obtain millimetres. Interface (0) or intraslab (1) coefficients are selected using Earthquake Type. Displacement coefficients are selected by Ts ≤ 0.1 s or Ts > 0.1 s; the probability-of-zero coefficients use Ts ≤ 0.6 s or Ts > 0.6 s.
